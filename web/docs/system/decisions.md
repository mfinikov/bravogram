# Decisions

## Description
Settled questions about the Bravogram landing page's design system. Precedence, highest first: the owner's direct words, then this page, then the foundation pages, then the component specs, then the code. To change a decision, edit it here and fix every page and component it names in the same change.

## Decisions
- D1 Typefaces: titles (h1 to h3 and the wordmark) are Familjen Grotesk at weight 500, and text is Inter at weight 400. Typography and Brand say so, and `app/layout.tsx` loads both through `next/font`.
- D2 Logo: the mark is the four-ellipse drawing in `components/logo.tsx`. Brand and Logo say so.
- D3 Tokens live in one hand-written block at the top of `app/globals.css`, each with its role in a comment. There is no token generator and no second token file: the page has one theme and no utility framework.
- D4 Existing token names stay (`--bg`, `--ink`, `--text`, `--soft`, `--line`, `--card`, `--panel`, `--accent`, `--accent-ink`, `--title`, `--body`, `--mono`). New tokens are named by job: `--font-size-{role}`, `--leading-{role}`, `--radius-{thing}`, `--graph-{kind}`, `--motion-{job}`. Space tokens are `--space-{pixels}`.
- D5 Token values equal the values the page used when the system was built, and nothing was merged or rounded, except the text colors in D8. Typography, Layout and Materials list the near-duplicates this leaves.
- D7 Direction of the redesign: few sections and few words, the existing type, column, cards and one accent, and a hero scene in one color drawn as line art from the Bravogram mark and a memory graph. HeroScene says so. The six graph colors stay only in the graph card, as the product's legend.
- D8 Small text passes 4.5:1 on white: `--soft` is black at 56% (4.95:1) and `--text` is black at 70% (8.59:1), so body text still reads darker than captions. Colors says so.
- D6 The page's sections (demo, cards, tiles, call to action, questions, nav, footer) stay plain markup in `app/page.tsx`. The registry holds InstallButton, Logo, HeroScene and TerminalDemo. Coverage gaps says how to add to each section.
- D9 Other tools' logos: the hero's "Works with" chips show each tool's own mark beside its name, because the owner asked for logos there (2026-10-03). They are files in `public/logos/`, 16px, with empty alt text since the name is beside them: Claude and MCP from simple-icons (CC0), Hermes from the hermes-agent repository (MIT), and a shell glyph drawn for this page. No other third-party mark appears on the page.
- D10 Direction, 2026-10-03, replacing the look in D7: the owner asked for the page to sit much closer to boat.dev. So the page is one open column with no rules between sections, the wordmark stands large in a field of broken horizontal lines with Bravogram marks floating in it, boxes are square with a 1.5px `--ink` border, the numbers are bars, the commands are a list, one bordered link leads to the docs, and the footer ends in a band of the same field. The accent is the darker blue `#1e40af`. The typefaces (D1), the mark (D2) and the copy stay Bravogram's own, and no drawing or asset is taken from boat.dev. D5 no longer holds for the values this changed.
- D11 Our own look, 2026-10-03: the owner asked for the page to be unique to Bravogram, so the borrowed signature is replaced by the product itself. The scene is a memory graph: dots in the six `--graph-*` colors of the real graph view, Bravogram marks as hubs, thin `--soft` links, around the wordmark in the hero and as a band in the footer. The headline highlight and the bars are pills, and the accent is Bravogram's `#2f5bff` again. This replaces the line field, the floating marks and the darker blue of D10, and lifts D7's limit of the graph colors to the graph card. The open column, the square bordered boxes and the section order of D10 stay.
